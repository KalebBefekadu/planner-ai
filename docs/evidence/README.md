# Engineering Evidence

Evidence documents explain what was inspected or experimentally proven. They support decisions but do not define current product behavior or delivery order.

- [Agent Native feasibility](agent-native-feasibility.md)
- [M0 spike register](spikes/README.md)

Keep dates, commands, pass criteria, limitations, and remaining gates explicit. Move stable outcomes into an ADR, architecture, or requirements document; do not let an experiment silently become product authority.
