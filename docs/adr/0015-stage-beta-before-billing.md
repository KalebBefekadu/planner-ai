# Stage beta before billing

Planner AI will move from owner dogfooding to a free invite-only beta of roughly 10 to 20 people, then to a wider capped beta. Billing is deferred until repeated usefulness and retention are demonstrated.

## Consequences

- Invite-only entry requires reliable authentication and onboarding, critical UI/chat parity, dry-run import, export, deletion, tested recovery, monitoring, and no open severity-one defects.
- Expansion requires four consecutive weeks within the core reliability objective plus successful restore and security reviews.
- Capture, Today, Notes, and Weekly Review completion are the primary workflow evidence for expansion and later pricing work.
