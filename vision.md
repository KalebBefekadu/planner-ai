# Planner AI Vision

Planner AI is a private, voice-first life planning and knowledge Workspace. It helps a person connect long-term direction to meaningful outcomes, concrete work, reflection, and durable Notes without forcing them to choose between a traditional interface and an AI assistant.

## The Product Promise

A person can always click, type, navigate, and review their own system. The assistant can perform the same authorized Operations through conversation, but it never becomes the only interface, a hidden source of truth, or an uncontrolled administrator.

Planner AI should make five things easier:

1. **Capture honestly:** Preserve thoughts as spoken or typed before interpretation.
2. **Plan coherently:** Connect Vision, yearly/quarterly Goals, and monthly/weekly Actions without confusing outcomes with work.
3. **Focus deliberately:** Make overload and rollover visible rather than rewarding an infinite backlog.
4. **Remember usefully:** Keep Notes, Reviews, and explicit Memory connected and portable.
5. **Use AI safely:** Let AI organize, explain, propose, and act through the same permissions, approvals, evidence, and Activity as the interface.

## Product Character

Planner AI is calm, direct, and serious about trust. It may challenge overcommitment, but it does not shame, manipulate, diagnose, or pretend to be a medical, legal, financial, or crisis professional. It is designed for adults managing personal planning and reflection.

## First Legitimate Release

The first release is:

- a responsive installable web application;
- cloud-authoritative with Supabase and complete export;
- private and single-owner per Workspace;
- usable without AI and resilient when AI providers fail;
- equipped with Today, Plan, Notes, Capture, Review, Activity, and a docked assistant;
- able to expose a narrow authenticated MCP surface;
- intentionally smaller than Notion and safer than an unrestricted agent platform.

It is not local-first, collaborative, a general database builder, a habit game, a native mobile suite, or a self-modifying production application.

## Long-Term Direction

After the core product proves reliability and repeated usefulness, Planner AI may add trusted calendar context, approved outbound integrations, stronger offline/local capabilities, native clients, richer extensions, and collaboration. Each expansion must preserve the same rule: new capabilities become explicit Planner AI Operations before they become AI powers.
